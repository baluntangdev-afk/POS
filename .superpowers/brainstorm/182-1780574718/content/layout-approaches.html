A — Persistent Split Panel

Product grid (left ~65%) + cart list (right ~35%) always visible. Tap a cart item to expand inline edit controls within the right panel — no dialog, no navigation. Panel switches between "list" and "edit" mode with animation.

B — Slide-Over Edit Panel

Product grid fills the full screen. Tapping a cart item (from a floating badge or cart icon) slides a panel in from the right edge, overlaying a portion of the grid. Grid stays visible for context. Panel dismisses on save/cancel.

C — Inline Edit Within Cart Panel

Persistent right panel shows the cart list. Tapping a cart item expands inline quantity + modifier controls directly inside the item row — no separate edit view. The panel stays in "list mode" at all times. Clean, dense, fast.

My recommendation

Option C — it keeps the cashier in one mental mode (the order panel is always the order panel), avoids the context-switch of a slide-over, and is the most touch-efficient pattern for a busy POS environment. Option A is the closest runner-up if full modifier editing visibility matters more.
