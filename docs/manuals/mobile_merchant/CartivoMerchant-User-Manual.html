1. About Cartivo Merchant

Cartivo Merchant (shown on the device as Cartivo Merchant, and titled DPO Merchant in some builds) is a companion app for third-party merchants who sell through the Cartivo / DPO platform. Its one job is to put every online order for your store in front of your staff the moment it is placed, and to let them move that order through to completion.

What the app does

  • Shows your live order feed. New orders from your storefront appear on the screen within a second, with a sound / banner alert.
  • Lets you work each order. Move an order from Pending to Preparing to Ready to Fulfilled, or cancel it.
  • Keeps a local copy. The orders you have already received are stored on the device, so the list still opens when the connection drops — you just can't receive new ones until it comes back.
  • Alerts you in the background. A phone notification fires for every new or changed order even when the app is not the screen you are looking at.

What the app does NOT do

  • It is not a point-of-sale / cash register. It does not ring up walk-in sales, take payment, or print receipts.
  • It has no product catalogue, no inventory, and no reports. Those live in the main Cartivo POS.
  • It has no user accounts or PINs. Anyone holding the unlocked device can work the order list. Treat the device accordingly.

The one thing to understand: this app needs the internet

Unlike the full Cartivo POS, this app does almost nothing offline. The live feed, order updates, and first-time setup all need a working Wi-Fi or mobile-data connection. Offline, you can only read the orders already downloaded.

Important  Keep the device on a stable connection during trading hours. The app reconnects on its own after a brief drop, but a long outage means missed orders.

2. Before you start

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet (Android 8 / 2017 or newer recommended; the app targets modern Android). iPhone / iPad is not supported.
  • The Cartivo Merchant app installed. Your IT person or provider builds and installs it — see the Technical Manual.
  • A Wi-Fi or mobile-data connection.
  • Your Merchant ID and Merchant Name, as issued by your Cartivo / DPO provider. If you were not given a Merchant ID, the app can generate one for you to hand back to your provider.
  • Someone on the merchant administrator side who can approve this device (see “Getting your device approved”).

How the app is laid out

There is only one screen: the dashboard. Everything happens there — a thin toolbar across the top, and the order list filling the rest. Setup prompts appear as pop-up dialogs over that screen.

AreaWhat it is
Top-leftThe Cartivo wordmark, and — once you are set up — your Merchant Name | Merchant ID underneath it.
Top-rightThe device-status icon (only while not yet approved), the live-connection pill, and the Settings gear.
Below the toolbarThe Orders heading, a Refresh link, and a row of filter tabs (All, plus one per status).
Main areaThe list of order cards, newest first. Pull down to refresh.

3. First-time setup — registering your merchant

The very first time the app opens with no merchant saved, it puts a Register Merchant dialog on screen that you cannot dismiss until it is filled in. This happens once per device.

  1. Open the app. The Register Merchant dialog appears.
  2. Check the Merchant ID field. The app has pre-filled a random 10-character ID. If your provider gave you an ID, delete the pre-filled one and type theirs exactly. If not, keep the generated one and pass it to your provider so they can register your store on their side.
  3. Type your Merchant Name — your store's name as you want it to read (for example Juan's Store).
  4. Tap Register. The button shows a spinner while the app talks to the server.
  5. If the details are accepted, the dialog closes and the app moves straight to checking whether this device is approved (next section).
  6. If you see a red error message instead, read it, fix the field it points to (usually a wrong Merchant ID or no connection), and tap Register again.
Important  The Merchant ID and Name you enter here are also what your provider sees. They must match what the provider has on file, or orders will never reach this device.

What “registering” actually does

Two things happen behind that one Register tap:

  • The app asks the server for a security token tied to your Merchant ID.
  • The app then enrols this physical device under your merchant account, sending a fingerprint of the device (its model, Android version, and a permanent install ID). The device starts life pending — it is not yet allowed to receive orders.

4. Getting your device approved

A newly registered device is Pending. It can open the app and show past orders, but the live feed stays switched off until a merchant administrator approves it from their side. You cannot approve your own device.

The three device states

StateIcon in toolbarWhat it means
PendingAmber hourglassSubmitted, waiting for the administrator. Live orders are off. You may keep using the app.
Approved(icon disappears)The device is cleared. Live orders turn on automatically — no restart needed.
DeactivatedRed block / no-entryThe administrator has turned this device's access off. Live orders are paused. Contact your administrator to restore it.

While you wait

  1. A Device Pending Approval dialog explains the status. Tap Got it to dismiss it — you can keep the app open.
  2. Ask your merchant administrator to approve the device (they do this in their own Cartivo admin tools).
  3. You do not need to do anything else. The app quietly re-checks every 15 seconds, and again each time you bring it back to the foreground. The moment approval lands, the amber icon vanishes, a green “Device approved — you're all set” banner appears, and the connection pill turns to Live.

Checking manually

If you don't want to wait for the automatic check:

  • Tap the amber hourglass icon in the toolbar, then tap Check again in the dialog. The button spins while it re-checks and reports the fresh result.
  • Or open Settings (the gear) — the card at the top of that screen shows the current device status and has its own Check status button.
Note  If the dialog shows a note from your administrator (a “review note”), read it — it usually says what they still need from you before approving.

6. The Orders list

The main area is the order list — one card per order, most recent at the top. It updates itself as orders arrive and change.

The filter tabs

  • All is selected by default and shows every order.
  • Extra tabs appear automatically, one for each status that currently has orders — Pending, Preparing, Ready, Fulfilled, Cancelled. Each tab shows a count.
  • Tap a tab to show only that status. If a tab empties out (its last order moved on), the view falls back to All.

What one order card shows

  • Order # and its status pill (tap the pill to change status — see the next section).
  • A subtitle line: the customer name (or Guest), and how the order is handed over — On-site (with the facility name), Pickup, or Delivery.
  • A relative time — just now, 5m ago, 2h ago.
  • The line items — quantity, product name, and line price.
  • The Total, in your storefront's currency (shown with a ₱ symbol).
  • A CANCEL ORDER button, unless the order is already Fulfilled or Cancelled.

Refreshing the list

  • The list refreshes itself on every live event. You rarely need to.
  • To force a full re-fetch from the server: tap the Refresh link next to the Orders heading, or pull down on the list.
  • If a refresh fails (no connection), a red “Couldn't refresh — showing cached data” banner appears with a Retry link. The list keeps showing the last data it had.

Empty and error states

You seeIt means
“No orders yet” with a receipt iconThe current tab has no orders. Normal at the start of the day.
“Couldn't load orders” with a Try Again buttonThe first load failed and there was no cached copy to fall back on. Check the connection and tap Try Again.

7. Working an order — status flow

The heart of the app: moving each order through its stages so the customer and your storefront can see progress.

The stages

StatusColourUse it when
PendingAmberThe order has just come in and nobody has started it. This is where new orders land.
PreparingAmberSomeone has begun making the order.
ReadyGreenThe order is finished and waiting for pickup / hand-off / the runner.
FulfilledTealThe customer has received the order. This is the end state — the card locks.
CancelledRedThe order will not be completed. Also an end state — the card locks.

Changing the status

  1. On the order card, tap the status pill (top-right of the card). It has a small down-arrow to show it is tappable.
  2. A short menu drops down: Pending, Preparing, Ready, Fulfilled. The current status has a check mark.
  3. Tap the status you want. The pill shows a spinner while it saves.
  4. When it succeeds the pill updates and the change is pushed to your storefront. If it fails, a red message slides up from the bottom and the pill stays on the old status — try again.
Note  You can move an order in any direction (for example back from Ready to Preparing) while it is not yet fulfilled or cancelled. Once it is Fulfilled or Cancelled the pill is no longer tappable.

Cancelling an order

  1. Tap the red CANCEL ORDER button at the bottom of the card.
  2. A confirmation asks “Cancel this order?” — it warns this can't be undone.
  3. Tap Cancel order to confirm, or Keep order to back out.
  4. On confirm, the order moves to Cancelled, the card locks, and your storefront is notified.

If another device changes the same order

Orders are shared. If a colleague on another device (or your storefront) changes an order, this app receives that change on the live feed and updates the card by itself — you may see a pill change under your finger. The most recent change always wins.

8. Order details view

Tap anywhere on an order card (except the status pill or the cancel button) to slide up a full, read-only detail sheet.

What the detail sheet adds over the card

  • The customer's email address, if the order carries one.
  • The district / delivery area name, if present.
  • Each line item with its unit price (“₱x each”) as well as the line total.
  • A clear Total at the bottom.
  1. Tap the card body.
  2. The sheet rises from the bottom and covers up to ~85% of the screen. Scroll inside it if the item list is long.
  3. Swipe it back down, or tap the dimmed area above it, to close. Nothing on this sheet can be edited — change status from the card instead.

Turning notifications on

  1. The first time the app runs it asks for permission to post notifications. Tap Allow.
  2. If you tapped Don't allow by mistake, open Android Settings › Apps › Cartivo Merchant › Notifications and switch them back on.
  3. Keep the Live orders channel set to high importance so alerts pop up and make a sound.

10. Merchant Settings

Tap the gear in the toolbar to open Merchant Settings. This is where you review the device status and change your store details.

What's on the settings screen

  • Device status card at the top — the current state (Waiting for approval / Device approved / Device deactivated), your merchant name, an explanation, and a Check status button.
  • Merchant ID field.
  • Merchant Name field.
  • Cancel and Save buttons.

Changing your store name only

  1. Open Settings, edit Merchant Name, tap Save.
  2. A green “Merchant settings saved” banner confirms it. The name updates in the toolbar and on your storefront records. Your orders and device approval are untouched.

Switching to a different Merchant ID

Important  Only do this if your provider has genuinely issued you a new Merchant ID. Changing it is a full reset of the store link on this device.
  1. Open Settings, replace the Merchant ID with the new one, adjust the name if needed, tap Save.
  2. The app wipes this device's saved credentials for the old merchant and re-enrols the device under the new Merchant ID.
  3. The device goes back to Pending — your administrator must approve it again under the new merchant before live orders resume.
  4. The old merchant's orders are no longer shown.

11. When the connection drops

The app is built to ride out short outages without you doing anything. Here is exactly what happens and what you can still do.

SituationWhat the app doesWhat you can do
Brief Wi-Fi blipPill shows Reconnecting, retries with growing gaps (1s, 2s, 4s… up to 30s). Reconnects and replays missed orders automatically.Nothing. Wait for it.
Longer outageStays on Reconnecting. The list keeps showing the last known orders.Read existing orders. You cannot receive new orders or reliably save status changes — a change may fail with a red message.
Airplane mode / no data at allPill goes to Reconnecting then effectively idle; a refresh shows the cached data banner.Read cached orders only. Restore the connection to resume.
Connection returnsReconnects within seconds, pill returns to Live, missed orders stream in (without a notification storm).Carry on.
Important  Status changes and cancellations are not queued while offline. If a change fails, it did not happen — redo it once the pill is back to Live.

12. Your daily routine

Opening up

  1. Put the device on its stand / charger where staff can see it.
  2. Open Cartivo Merchant.
  3. Confirm the toolbar shows your store name and the pill reads Live (green). If it doesn't, see Troubleshooting.
  4. Do a quick pull-to-refresh to be sure the list is current.

During trading

  • Work new orders from the top of the Pending tab.
  • Move each order to Preparing when work starts, Ready when it's done, Fulfilled when the customer has it.
  • Cancel only with a real reason — it can't be undone and the customer is told.
  • Glance at the pill now and then. Green = fine.

Closing down

  1. Check the Pending and Preparing tabs are empty (or deal with what's left).
  2. Leave the device charging and connected — there is no “sign out” and nothing to back up. The app can stay open.

13. Troubleshooting & FAQ

The pill never leaves “Connecting” or “Reconnecting”

  1. Check the device is actually online — open a web page in the browser.
  2. Check the device status: if the toolbar shows an amber hourglass, the device is still Pending — it needs administrator approval, not a connection fix.
  3. If it shows a red block icon, the device is Deactivated — contact your administrator.
  4. Pull down to refresh. If that fails too, close the app fully and re-open it.
  5. Still stuck: hand the Technical Manual to your IT person — the server address in the app's configuration may be wrong or unreachable.

The pill says “Offline” and won't change

“Offline” means the app isn't even trying, which points to setup rather than the network: no merchant registered, the device not approved, or the device deactivated. Open Settings and read the device-status card.

I registered but no orders ever arrive

  1. Confirm the Merchant ID in Settings exactly matches what your provider registered — no extra spaces, right case.
  2. Confirm the device shows Live (approved + connected).
  3. Place a test order on your storefront and watch for it.
  4. If it still doesn't appear, your storefront may not be linked to this Merchant ID on the provider's side — raise it with them.

A status change failed

You'll see a red message at the bottom of the screen and the pill won't have moved. This is almost always a momentary connection problem. Wait for the pill to read Live, then try the change again.

I got a burst of notifications for old orders

This can happen after a long time closed or a long outage. It's harmless — the app is re-syncing. The 4-second quiet window usually suppresses it; a very large backlog can still leak a few.
